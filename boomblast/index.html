Bloomblast / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Bloomblast (Android package com.zenyne.bloomblast), developed by Rasla, handles information when you play the game or contact us.

In short: Bloomblast has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or in-app purchases, and it does not connect to any server run by us or by a third party. Your scores and settings stay on your device. When you choose to play co-op, the game talks only to other devices on your own Wi-Fi network.

Information the game uses

Scores, stats and settings on your device

The game saves your top 10 runs (score, level reached, solo or co-op, and the date), simple play statistics such as runs started and best level, your sound, music, vibration and screen-shake settings, and the optional player name you type in your profile. These are stored only in the game's private app storage on your device. They are never uploaded.

Same Wi-Fi co-op

Co-op works only between devices on the same local Wi-Fi network or hotspot. To let nearby players find a game, a host device broadcasts a short message on the local network containing the game name, a display name and the number of players in the lobby. The display name is the player name you set in your profile; if you leave it empty, the device's model name (for example “Pixel 8”) is used instead.

During a match, the devices connect directly to each other using their local network addresses and exchange the game data needed to play together: your display name, player positions, actions, health, pick-ups and scores. This traffic stays on your local network and is not sent to us. We do not receive or store it.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to help with your request.

Sharing and third parties

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or social SDKs.

Other people on the same Wi-Fi network who have Bloomblast open can see a hosted game's display name in their list of games, and players in your match see your display name. Only host or join games on networks and with people you trust: local co-op traffic is not encrypted.

Google Play, which delivers the app, handles information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

  • Network access and Wi-Fi state: used only to find and connect to co-op games on your local network.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback when you take damage. You can turn it off in Settings.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

How long information is kept

  • Local saves: remain on your device until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it.
  • Co-op data: exists only while a match is running and is not saved after it ends.
  • Emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. Ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

  • Play Solo to keep the game entirely offline.
  • Set your own player name in the profile so your device model name is never shown to other players.
  • Clear the game's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the game, to delete your scores, stats and settings.

Because we hold no game data about you, there is no account to delete. For any privacy question or request, email 0xrasla@gmail.com with the subject “Bloomblast privacy request.”

Children

Bloomblast does not ask for age, real names or any contact details, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's features change, for example by adding online play or ads, we will update this policy before that release and change the date above.
