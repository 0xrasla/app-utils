Bloom Blast / Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Bloom Blast: Pixel Survivor (Android package com.rasla.bloomblast), developed by Rasla, handles information when you play the game or contact us.

In short: Bloom Blast is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad services receive device and usage information to show ads, as described below. We run no servers and have no accounts, analytics or in-app purchases. Your scores and settings stay on your device, and co-op talks only to other devices on your own Wi-Fi network.

Information the game uses

Scores, stats and settings on your device

The game saves your top 10 runs (score, level reached, solo or co-op, and the date), simple play statistics such as runs started and best level, your sound, music, vibration and screen-shake settings, and the optional player name you type in your profile. These are stored only in the game's private app storage on your device. They are never uploaded.

Same Wi-Fi co-op

Co-op works only between devices on the same local Wi-Fi network or hotspot. To let nearby players find a game, a host device broadcasts a short message on the local network containing the game name, a display name and the number of players in the lobby. The display name is the player name you set in your profile; if you leave it empty, the device's model name (for example “Pixel 8”) is used instead.

During a match, the devices connect directly to each other using their local network addresses and exchange the game data needed to play together: your display name, player positions, actions, health, pick-ups and scores. This traffic stays on your local network and is not sent to us. We do not receive or store it.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and to help with your request.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob in solo play: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (to revive or get a power-up), and occasional full-screen ads between runs. Ads are never shown during co-op matches.

To deliver, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, including your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic data. Depending on your consent and device settings, ads may be personalised. This information is sent to Google, not to us; we only see aggregated ad reports.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal information, and we do not receive or store information about you ourselves. The only third-party SDKs in the game are Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (for consent), described above. There are no analytics or social SDKs.

Other people on the same Wi-Fi network who have Bloomblast open can see a hosted game's display name in their list of games, and players in your match see your display name. Only host or join games on networks and with people you trust: local co-op traffic is not encrypted.

Google Play, which delivers the app, handles information under Google's Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

  • Network access and Wi-Fi state: used to load ads and to find and connect to co-op games on your local network.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google's ad services as described in the Ads section. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback when you take damage. You can turn it off in Settings.

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

How long information is kept

  • Local saves: remain on your device until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it.
  • Ad data: retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Co-op data: exists only while a match is running and is not saved after it ends.
  • Emails: kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. Ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

  • Play with your device offline (solo works without internet) to stop ads from loading.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional; skip the offer button if you do not want to watch one.
  • Use Settings → Ad privacy in the game (where required), or your Android ad settings, to change ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID.
  • Set your own player name in the profile so your device model name is never shown to other players.
  • Clear the game's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the game, to delete your scores, stats and settings.

Because we hold no game data about you, there is no account to delete. Requests about ad data can be made to Google through the links above. For any privacy question or request, email 0xrasla@gmail.com with the subject “Bloomblast privacy request.”

Children

Bloom Blast is not directed at children under 13. It does not ask for age, real names or any contact details, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's features change, for example by adding online play, we will update this policy before that release and change the date above.
