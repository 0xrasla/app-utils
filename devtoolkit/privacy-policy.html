DevKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This privacy policy explains how DevKit ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the application.

Summary

DevKit is a developer utility toolkit. It is designed to process data locally on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Data Processing

All tools included in DevKit — such as JSON formatting, Base64 encoding, JWT decoding, hash generation, UUID generation, timestamp conversion, regex testing, color conversion, QR generation, and password generation — process the content you enter entirely on your device. None of this content is sent to our servers, because we operate no servers. Input data never leaves your device.

Local Storage

DevKit stores only a minimal amount of local data on your device to remember your preferences. This includes:

  • Your selected color theme
  • Whether you have unlocked the "Pro" set of themes

This data is stored locally on your device using platform-standard preferences and is not transmitted anywhere. You can clear it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Permissions

DevKit does not require any sensitive permissions, such as access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photos.

Third-Party Services

DevKit does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or other services that collect data.

Children's Privacy

DevKit does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected, no special protections beyond the local-processing design described above are required.

Security

Because your data never leaves your device, there is no risk of it being intercepted during transmission to a server. We recommend you only use DevKit to process information you are comfortable handling locally, as with any offline developer tool.

Data Deletion

To remove all locally stored data, simply uninstall the app or clear its app data through your device's settings. There is no server-side data to delete.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
