Better Buy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 · App version 1.0.0

Better Buy helps you compare grocery pack prices. This policy explains how the Android app with package name com.zenyne.betterbuy handles information and how to contact its developer about privacy.

Information stays on your device

Better Buy stores your current draft, saved comparisons, product names, entered prices and quantities, selected offers, comparison names and settings in the app's private storage. These values are used to calculate unit prices, restore your draft and reopen saved comparisons.

The app does not collect or transmit this information to the developer or third parties. It has no accounts, backend, advertising SDK, analytics, crash-reporting service or network service. All calculation and saving features work offline.

The app reads your device's language and region settings locally to choose initial currency and number formatting, and follows your device's light or dark appearance. It does not transmit these settings or track your activity. We do not sell or rent your comparison data.

Local storage and deletion

Data remains in the app's private storage until you change or delete it; there is no automatic expiry. You can delete saved comparisons from Saved. If a deleted comparison is also open as your current draft, the draft remains until you change it or start a new comparison. Starting a new comparison replaces the current draft without deleting other saved comparisons.

To remove all local drafts, comparisons and preferences, open Android Settings, find Better Buy under Apps, and choose Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device), or uninstall Better Buy. There is no account to delete and no server copy for the developer to remove.

Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for app data. There is no cloud restore or export in this version. The developer cannot recover comparisons after deletion. Device security and access to your unlocked device remain under your control.

Permissions and other services

Better Buy does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or shared files, and does not use an advertising identifier. Private app storage does not require a shared-storage permission.

Google Play and Android may process information independently to provide their store, app delivery or device services under their own policies. Such services are separate from Better Buy's local comparisons.

Data security

Comparison data is stored in the app's private directory, which Android normally isolates from other apps. Better Buy does not add a separate password or its own encryption to this file. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep its operating system updated. Anyone who can use the app on your unlocked device may be able to view your comparisons.

If you contact support

If you voluntarily email support, the support contact will receive your email address and anything you include. That correspondence is used to respond to your enquiry and is separate from app storage. Do not send sensitive information or comparisons you do not want the support contact to see.

Support correspondence is handled through email services and retained for as long as needed to resolve your enquiry and meet applicable legal record-keeping obligations. You can contact the address below to request deletion of correspondence you have sent, subject to those obligations. Support cannot access or delete data stored on your device.

Children's privacy

Better Buy is a shopping utility for general audiences and is not directed at children. The app does not ask users of any age to submit personal information and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the version above. If future versions add network services or advertising, the policy and applicable store disclosures will be updated to explain their data practices.
